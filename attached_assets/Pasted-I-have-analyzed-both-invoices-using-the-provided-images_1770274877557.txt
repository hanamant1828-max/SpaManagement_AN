I have analyzed both invoices using the provided images and backend code and identified inconsistencies in the tax calculation logic.

Findings from the Invoices

Invoice 1 (Gel Manicure & Shampoo)

Services are treated as GST Exclusive (GST added on top of the base price).

Gel Manicure base price is ₹35.00, with 15% GST (₹5.25) added, resulting in ₹40.25.

The grand total is ₹55.00, but the subtotal before GST shows ₹49.66, indicating a calculation mismatch.

Invoice 2 (Shampoo & Cleanup)

Products (Shampoo) are treated as GST Inclusive, where GST is extracted from the MRP.

MRP ₹20.00 → Base ₹17.39 + GST ₹2.61 (15%).

Services (Cleanup) are treated as GST Exclusive.

Base ₹250.00 + GST ₹37.50 = ₹287.50.

The displayed grand total is ₹270.00, which is less than the service total alone, clearly indicating a major calculation issue in either the template or backend logic.

Root Cause Analysis

Services are currently handled as GST Exclusive.

Products are handled as GST Inclusive.

This inconsistency leads to incorrect subtotals and grand totals.

Additionally, the variable total_gst_rate was missing in the invoice update route, causing errors during edit mode.

Resolution Implemented

Unified GST Logic: Both services and products are now treated as GST Inclusive. GST is consistently extracted from the MRP for all items.

Backend Fix: Updated both invoice creation and edit routes to use the same tax calculation logic and ensured all required variables (including total_gst_rate) are properly defined.

Template Update: Modified the invoice print template to display both services and products as “GST Inclusive.”

Calculation Correction: Fixed subtotal and tax summary calculations in the invoice footer to ensure they accurately reflect the itemized data.

Final Outcome

The invoice module now behaves consistently across create, edit, and save & print modes.
Both services and products follow the same GST Inclusive model, ensuring that the listed price is exactly what the customer pays and that all totals are calculated correctly.
